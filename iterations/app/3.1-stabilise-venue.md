### 3.2 Crash sweep

### 3.3 Permissions on judges' phone types
| Platform | Permission | When | Reason line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Android | Location (while using) | First walk | "So we can draw your walk on the map." |
| Android | Location (background) | First walk, after the foreground prompt | "So your walk keeps recording when the screen is off." |
| Android 13+ | Notifications | After onboarding | "So we can remind you and ask how you are." |
| Android | Camera | First warm-up | "So we can count your movements. Nothing is recorded." |
| iPhone (Expo Go) | Location (while using), notifications (local) | Same moments | Same reasons |

### 3.4 Judge QR codes (on the slide)
1. **Android** → APK download (EAS preview build).
2. **iPhone** → "Install Expo Go, then scan" → EAS Update `demo`.
3. **WhatsApp QR** and the **phone number** for judges who install nothing (owned by the Voice / WhatsApp tracks).

### 3.5 Judge account experience
- Opening via the judge link skips onboarding and lands on Home with a **pending check-in** and **a walk tomorrow**, so the judge can do "No → join walk" right away.
- Coordinate with backend: Judge 1…10 accounts, each individually resettable.

## 8. Outcome (Thu 12 Nov 2026)

**Done in code** (branch `sarath`)
- **Judge accounts (§3.5).** Judge 1 to Judge 10 are seeded on every reset: set up (Bishan,
  Singpass-verified) with nothing done yet, so Home shows today's check-in and tomorrow's
  morning walk, and "No → join walk" works straight away. `POST /demo/judges/{n}/reset` resets
  one judge and leaves everyone else alone (demo servers only).
- **Judge link (§3.4).** Opening `/judge/3` (APK: `bricks-preview://judge/3`) resets Judge 3,
  signs this phone in as them, skips onboarding and opens Home ("Welcome, Judge 3."). If the
  server can't be reached it says so, with Try again. Any other number goes to the normal start.
  The Guest Judge in the stage switcher still goes through sign-in and onboarding.
- **Check connection (§3.1).** Settings → Demo → Check connection says how fast the server
  answered (slow above 1.5 s: "try the hotspot"), or that it can't be reached, or that its
  database didn't answer. For testing venue Wi-Fi against the hotspot.
- **Permissions (§3.3), checked:** location only when a walk starts, background location right
  after (once), notifications at the onboarding reminders step or when making a reminder,
  contacts only when adding to the Safe Circle. All with plain reasons (`app.config.ts`). There
  is no camera use yet, so no camera prompt.
- **Crash sweep (§3.2):** a test opens every screen (43, onboarding included) and fails on any
  crash. Fixed a test helper that kept the demo account between tests.
- **Checks:** app 600 tests, tsc, eslint, prettier, expo-doctor 20/20, Android export; backend
  ruff, mypy, 262 tests.

**Judge QR codes (for the slide)** — one per judge, numbers 1 to 10
- Android, step 1: the APK download link from the EAS preview build.
- Android, step 2: `bricks-preview://judge/<n>` (opens the installed app as Judge n).
- iPhone (Expo Go): the `demo` update's Expo Go link with `/--/judge/<n>` on the end, e.g.
  `exp://u.expo.dev/<project id>/group/<update group id>/--/judge/3`. **Check this exact form on
  a phone once the update is published**; without the `/--/judge/<n>` part the app opens at
  onboarding, and Settings → Demo still works.
- Scanning the same judge's code again starts that judge over (only theirs).
