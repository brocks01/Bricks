## 3. Tasks

### 3.1 Network
- Run the golden path on **venue Wi-Fi** and on a **phone hotspot**. Note the latency of `/assistant/turn` and the walk upload.
- Captive portal / blocked websockets → make the hotspot the default for all demo phones.

### 3.2 Crash sweep
- Open every P0 screen on every team phone; fix **crashes only**.

### 3.3 Permissions on judges' phone types
| Platform | Permission | When | Reason line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Android | Location (while using) | First walk | "So we can draw your walk on the map." |
| Android | Location (background) | First walk, after the foreground prompt | "So your walk keeps recording when the screen is off." |
| Android 13+ | Notifications | After onboarding | "So we can remind you and ask how you are." |
| Android | Camera | First warm-up | "So we can count your movements. Nothing is recorded." |
| iPhone (Expo Go) | Location (while using), notifications (local) | Same moments | Same reasons |

### 3.4 Judge QR codes (on the slide)
1. **Android** → APK download (EAS preview build).
2. **iPhone** → "Install Expo Go, then scan" → EAS Update `demo`.
3. **WhatsApp QR** and the **phone number** for judges who install nothing (owned by the Voice / WhatsApp tracks).

### 3.5 Judge account experience
- Opening via the judge link skips onboarding and lands on Home with a **pending check-in** and **a walk tomorrow**, so the judge can do "No → join walk" right away.
- Coordinate with backend: Judge 1…10 accounts, each individually resettable.
