## 3. Feature specs

### 3.1 S24 Walk history & trends (Apple Health trends + Strava log)
- **Week · Month** as two big buttons.
- `StatCard` "8.6 km this week" + "4 walks · 2 h 10 min".
- Bar chart: distance per day (week) / per week (month), one colour, plain labels (`react-native-svg`).
- Comparison sentence: "1.2 km more than last week". When lower: "A lighter week. A walk today?" Never negative.
- Personal bests card: Longest walk · Most steps in a day · Walks this month (labelled badges).
- Walk list: route thumbnail, date, place, km → S23.
- Read-out: "This week you walked 8.6 kilometres in 4 walks. 1.2 kilometres more than last week."
- **Avoid:** multi-metric charts, pace graphs, red/green.

### 3.2 S19 Group walk feed + Cheers (Strava kudos → Cheer)
- S19 "Recent group walks": route thumbnail (public polyline), "9 people · 3.1 km · Saturday", walker avatars, **Cheer** (tap) + **Voice cheer** (record ≤ 10 s → `VoiceNoteBubble`).
- S23 **Share to my Circle** → sets `visibility='circle'`; the owner sees the cheers on S23.
- No leaderboards, no rankings. Golden path step 3: "Lim cheers it".

### 3.3 S13 Simple Mode (Be My Eyes + Grab)
- Toggle in S40 and on S10 (family can set it).
- 3 full-width buttons, each ~25% of screen height: **Today** (reads today's plan + check-in YesNo) · **Talk** (Buddy) · **Help** (red → S28). Auto-read on.

### 3.4 S11 Admin: members & QR (Luma manage guests)
- Org name + verified badge · **Show QR** (full-screen) · Share WhatsApp join link · Upload list · Members: name + status (Joined / Invited / Needs help).
- No chart dashboards. Admin never sees the health card or Safe Circle.
- Demo: project the QR; 3–5 phones scan → status becomes Joined.

### 3.5 S25 Move Along polish
- One-colour skeleton overlay: blue, green on a good rep, **never red**.
- Safety stop end-to-end ("Let's stop here and rest." + Done; saved with `safety_stopped`).
- Second exercise: seated knee extension.
- All cues via `posture.cue.*` in 4 languages, spoken in the Read It Out voice.
- Finish card: "10 sit-to-stands. Nice and steady." (+ "Getting a little tired near the end" if fatigued). No scores or numbers.
- iPhone: camera-free guided warm-up: each move read aloud with a photo, tap **Done**.

### 3.6 Others if time allows
- **S27 Health Card:** blood group, conditions, medicines, emergency contacts; "Only you and your family can see this."; family can edit.
- **S35 Life Stories:** story list (title, length, play) + record (prompt suggestions, "Ask Grandpa" questions).
- **S10 family overview:** this week's walks, check-in streak, next event (only what the senior shares) + buttons.
