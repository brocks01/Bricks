## 2. Writing rules
- Utility category: neutral, helpful, **not promotional** (that wording passes review).
- First words say what it is. Short lines. **No emojis.** Bold for the key line (`*text*`).
- Variables as `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`… with sample values for review.
- Footer on every template: "Reply STOP to pause messages."
- Quick-reply buttons ≤ 3, each ≤ 20 characters.
