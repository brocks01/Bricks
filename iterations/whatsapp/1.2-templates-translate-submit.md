### 2.2 Submit
- Meta: WhatsApp Manager → Message templates → Create, or via the Graph API:
```bash
curl -X POST "https://graph.facebook.com/v20.0/$WABA_ID/message_templates" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{
  "name": "daily_checkin", "language": "ta", "category": "UTILITY",
  "components": [
    {"type":"BODY","text":"*காலை வணக்கம் {{1}}.* நேற்று நடந்தீர்களா?","example":{"body_text":[["ராஜ்"]]}},
    {"type":"FOOTER","text":"..."},
    {"type":"BUTTONS","buttons":[{"type":"QUICK_REPLY","text":"ஆம்"},{"type":"QUICK_REPLY","text":"இல்லை"}]}]}'
```
- Script it (`scripts/submit_templates.py`) from `templates.py` so resubmits after a rejection are quick.

## 3. 24-hour rule reminder (`06` §5.2)
- Member messages us → free-form for 24 h. Outside that window → approved templates only.
- All scheduled sends (check-in, reminders, invites, SOS alerts to helpers, family alerts) are templates.
- The daily check-in reply opens a fresh 24 h window for follow-ups.
