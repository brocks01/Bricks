## 2. Slide content

**Title:** "Your WhatsApp group stays. We add the safety net and the purpose."

- **No migration:** each Circle stores its existing WhatsApp **group invite link** (pasted by the captain). Members keep chatting where they already are. We don't do in-app chat on purpose.
- **What we add on top:** daily Yes / No check-in, "No" → a real walk nearby, reminders, Safe Circle SOS, thank-yous for people who teach, weekly progress cards families can forward.
- **Three doors, one brain:** the same member can use the app, WhatsApp or a phone call; one profile, one memory.
- **Onboarding at scale:** RCs / centres show a QR at a session → residents join via WhatsApp → each confirms with one tap (consent) → auto-joined to the centre's Circle.
- **Rules we respect:** opt-in recorded, STOP / START, approved Utility templates, 24-hour window, no ads.

## 3. 20-second answer (practice line)
> "We don't replace WhatsApp groups. Seniors keep them. Each Circle links to its existing group. What WhatsApp alone can't do is notice when someone goes quiet, turn a 'no' into a walk with neighbours, and alert family when help is needed. That's what we add, on the same WhatsApp they already use."
