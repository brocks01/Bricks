# Wisdom: our core philosophy

Version 4, 16 Nov 2026. This page is the root of everything else. If a feature, a screen or a sentence in another document does not serve what is written here, change it.

## The one thing we are solving
> **Social isolation and loneliness in people who are retiring or retired.**

Everything else (the tabs, the agent, the app, the numbers) is a means. Every feature must pass one test: **does it put a real person in touch with another real person, regularly, in a way that makes them feel they belong?** If not, it is decoration.

## Who we are
A small student team building **Wisdom** for the **Singapore–India Hackathon 2026** (raiSE, Social Innovation and Inclusive Development). We build one thing: a system that connects generations, so that nobody spends their later years alone and unasked, and what a lifetime taught reaches the people who need it before it is lost.

## Our motto
> **Every life has something to teach.**

*(Proposed; the owner confirms or changes it. Supporting line: "Wisdom connects generations to live a whole life together.")*

## The feeling at the centre
Loneliness is not a lack of activities. It is a lack of **belonging**, and belonging is a feeling, not a skill or an interest.

The owner puts it plainly: *"I am from Bihar. Chhath Puja is being celebrated. I am in Singapore, in a five-star hotel, which every Indian dreams of. And I am not happy, because my family and my community are together, engaged in the festival, and I am not one of them. I do not want a fancy place. I want to be there."* If a young person feels that for a week, with a phone full of contacts, imagine an older person who feels it every day: the phone that does not ring, the meals alone, the afternoon with no one who says their name, a family that loves them but is busy, nobody who would notice if they were gone.

This pain is **invisible**. People say "I'm fine." They do not want to be a burden. That is why the first rule of Wisdom is: **we must reach them; they will not come to us.**

## What a person needs (our four rules)
Everything we build serves at least one of these.
1. **To be noticed.** A voice at a predictable time that remembers yesterday: "How was your knee? Did your grandson call?"
2. **To be needed.** Someone asks them something, and their answer matters: "A student wants to hear how you fixed engines."
3. **To be expected.** A circle that would notice if they did not come.
4. **To be remembered.** Their story is kept, and someone younger will carry it on.

## The Uncle Tan test
Before any feature, ask: *"Would this make a person like Uncle Tan (62, retired after forty years, alone at home on a Monday) feel noticed, needed, expected or remembered, and could he use it without help?"* If the answer is no, we do not build it, however clever it is.

## Backbone and doors
- **The backbone** is the relationships (who belongs to whom, who showed up, who was asked), one agent with one memory, and the notification system. It all lives in the **backend**.
- **The doors** are how a person reaches the backbone: a phone call, WhatsApp, a text message, Telegram, the app. The **app is one door, not the product.** Each door is a thin adapter; none holds any logic.
- The test: **a person who never installs the app must still get the full benefit.** We build the backbone and the philosophy first, then add the doors one by one.

## What we believe (principles)
1. **Nobody is only a recipient.** An older person is a giver first: of experience, of time, of care. We never treat them as a patient or a charity case.
2. **Belonging is felt.** It comes from identity and shared life (where I am from, what we keep, who I live beside), not only from shared hobbies.
3. **Showing up beats scrolling.** We count check-ins, conversations and who was present, not clicks. There is no feed.
4. **Reach out, with consent.** The system takes the first step (a call, a message, a nudge) at a time and in a language the person chose, never at night, never nagging.
5. **Be needed, not pitied.** The message is never "you must be lonely". It is "I wanted to ask you something."
6. **Real bonds across generations, in both directions.** Elders pass on experience; the next generation learns it and becomes their support system. The gap between generations, and the slow loss of culture, closes only when both give.
7. **Dignity in earning, and no money held.** Experience may be shared free or paid, the elder's choice. We never hold or move money.
8. **A ring of people, not one voice.** The agent introduces people to people and steps back. It must never replace human contact.
9. **Safe by design.** The other side always decides; nobody is forced into a conversation. Stories are private until shared. Young people confirm they are 15 or older. Risky actions need a clear yes. A phone number alone never unlocks anything risky. The family hears about a person only with that person's consent, except when something may be wrong.
10. **Honest about what we are.** The agent says it is an AI, gives no medical advice, and in an emergency says to call 995 (Singapore) or 112 (India).

## What we are not
Not a social network, not a care or medical service, not an event organiser, not a community builder of our own, not an advertising business. We do not sell people's data.

## How we solve it: three products, three pains
Wisdom is really **three products**, each solving a different pain. They are linked, but each stands on its own.

| Product | The pain | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Communities** (the heart) | *I do not belong.* | **Belonging, based on emotion, not on interest or skill.** An elderly person does not want to meet "someone who codes"; they want to feel they are among their own. A circle can be about where we are from, what we keep, where we live, who we were. It can be **men only, women only, or open to all**, and a circle that already exists (for example at an Active Ageing Centre) can be **brought in**. |
| **Wisdom** | *I am not heard. I feel I am worth nothing.* | **To be heard and valued.** A person shares a lifetime of experience in **three domains: cultural, social and professional**, free or paid (their choice), with anyone who wants it, of any age. The next generation learns, and gives support back. |
| **Marketplace** | *There is nothing for me to go to, nowhere to be useful.* | **The physical world nearby.** Events at a mall, an Active Ageing Centre or any third party, where a person can **earn, contribute, volunteer, or simply enjoy and be there**. |

**How they link.** Communities is the heart; the other two feed it and are fed by it. A circle goes together to an event (Communities and Marketplace). An elder from a circle is asked for her cultural story (Communities and Wisdom). A person who volunteers at an event is heard afterwards (Marketplace and Wisdom). But they are different products with different pains, and the lines between them stay clear:
- *Do I belong with these people?* is Communities.
- *Am I heard, am I worth something?* is Wisdom.
- *Where can I go, near me, to earn, help or enjoy?* is the Marketplace.

**We are not an event provider and not a community creator.** We **bring people to what already exists**: communities and events run by Active Ageing Centres, malls and third parties, and communities people start themselves. Our value is that the person is **noticed, welcomed and expected** when they arrive.

Around the three: the **agent** is the front door on every channel (welcomes, learns what a person wants, writes down their stories, reminds, nudges, connects), and the **profile** decides what a person sees. The app is one door among several.

## Our three pillars, and what makes us different
- **Heart (Communities):** where you feel you belong.
- **Wisdom (experience sharing):** where you feel heard, valued and able to contribute back to society.
- **Marketplace (exchange):** where you exchange value and earn.

**Interests can connect people, but belonging makes them feel at home.** We are not another Instagram. Instagram understands a person and connects them; we do that too, and then go where it cannot: we bring people **physically together**, we give their **experience** a place to be heard and passed on, and we help them **earn**. That combination is our unique strength.

The logo says the same thing: two people, an elder in green and a younger person in amber, in one embrace. Interchange is the idea: experience, belonging and value flow in both directions between generations.

## How we know it is working
A person feels less lonely (a short three-question check at the start and at 3 and 6 months), and the leading signs: someone leaves the house tomorrow, an older person is asked something, a real conversation happens between generations, a market-day stall has someone behind it who last month sat at home.
