# Three products, three pains (requirement analysis)

Root: `../01-philosophy/philosophy.md`. Updated 16 Nov 2026 from the owner's direction. If another page disagrees with this one about the tabs, this page wins.

## The model in one table
Wisdom is three products, each solving a different pain, linked but distinct. The bottom bar has **four tabs: Home, Communities, Marketplace and Wisdom** (the server's profile decides whether the last two show). Communities is the heart of the system.

| | Communities | Wisdom | Marketplace |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The pain** | I do not belong. | I am not heard; I feel I am worth nothing. | There is nothing near me to go to; nowhere to be useful. |
| **What it gives** | Belonging, by emotion, not interest or skill. | To be heard and valued: a lifetime of experience shared. | The physical world nearby: where to earn, contribute, volunteer, or just enjoy and be there. |
| **Unit** | A circle of people | An experience (cultural, social or professional) and a conversation | An opportunity: an event, a market day, a volunteering slot, a paid gig, a show |
| **Made by** | People, or imported from an Active Ageing Centre | Elders (and the next generation, who give support) | Hosts: Active Ageing Centres, malls, third parties. **We are not the event provider.** |
| **Money** | None | Free or paid, the giver's choice; direct, never held by us | Whatever the host sets (a stall fee, a wage); direct, never held by us |
| **Built today** | Rhythm layer (events, check-ins, standing); belonging layer being built | Stories, guidance, requests, introductions, sessions; domains, paid and support being built | Market days with stalls; host-based nearby opportunities being built |

## How the three link
A circle goes together to an event (Communities ↔ Marketplace). An elder of a circle is asked for her cultural story (Communities ↔ Wisdom). A person who volunteered at an event is heard afterwards, and an elder who earned at a market day tells how she learned the craft (Marketplace ↔ Wisdom). Every link is a **choice the person makes**; nothing is forced.

## The four feelings every feature serves
Noticed, needed, expected, remembered (see the philosophy page). A feature that serves none does not get built.

## Decisions taken (16 Nov 2026)
1. Communities is the heart. It is **belonging by emotion**, not by interest or skill. A circle can be **men only, women only, or open to all** (the person's own declaration, opt-in), and communities that already exist (for example at an Active Ageing Centre) are **imported**, not recreated.
2. Wisdom has **three domains of experience: cultural, social, professional**. It can be **free or paid**, per offer, chosen by the giver. The learner is anyone 15 or older. The next generation **supports the elderly in return** (a hand with a phone, company on a walk, an errand).
3. The Marketplace is **physical and nearby**: events and opportunities hosted by Active Ageing Centres, malls and third parties, where a person can **earn, contribute, volunteer or enjoy**. We bring our users to events that already exist; we do not organise events. Our own market days are one kind of opportunity among others.
4. No platform fee at first; payment is always direct between people; we never hold money.
5. Trust comes from the person's own proof (field, years, where), **endorsements and counted conversations**; the system never gives medical, legal or financial advice.
6. Build the backend and the philosophy first; add the doors (phone, WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram, app) one by one. The app is one door.
